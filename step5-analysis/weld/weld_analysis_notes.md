## Assumptions (read first)

1. **Scope.** Weld County sites. A row is in scope when its county is stated by the source (water permits), its AIRS ID carries the 123 prefix (air permits; 14 rows whose county column says Weld but whose prefix is not 123 were left out as office addresses), its zip is wholly inside Weld County (Census 2020 ZCTA file, `weld_zip_share.json`, 26 zips wholly inside, 17 straddling), the Census geocoder places the address in Weld County (174 addresses looked up: 65 Weld, 44 Larimer, 17 Boulder, 9 Adams, 3 Broomfield, 36 no match), or the row comes from the Weld permit or planning layers (Frederick, Greeley, Fort Lupton and unincorporated Weld lie wholly in Weld). Rows with a Weld mailing city and no other basis are kept in a separate "county unconfirmed" section (23 rows). Rows in a split city (Windsor, Johnstown, Erie, Longmont, Brighton, Berthoud, Lochbuie, Thornton, Northglenn) that the geocoder places outside Weld were dropped (13 Mile High CRE rows), not kept as unconfirmed.
2. **The points table, word lists, normalisers and fit rules are Denver's, with one addition** (`v3_lib.PTS`): a named county tax incentive agreement scores 20, the same as a company-named construction stormwater permit, as a county record that names the company and the facility address before the equipment goes into service (Matthew's go, 2026-11-06, on the recommendation after the first pass). It is read only where the county config lists an incentive file (`LOCAL_INCENTIVE_FILES`), so Denver and Adams rebuild byte for byte (checked after every script change today). Nothing was tuned to the Weld ground truth.
3. **County layers.** Six of the nine Weld step 3 files are read through the county-layer loader: Frederick building permits as the permit layer (no valuation in the layer, so tier A is a construction permit type with an industrial word, as for Adams County), and the Weld planning cases, Weld site plan reviews, Frederick development applications, Greeley TRAKiT projects and Fort Lupton projects as the planning layer. The loader's holds apply: low-value permit types (signs, fire, racking, generators, trailers, demolition and the like) are held at B3b whatever step 3 said, commercial-use buildings at B3a, and cancelled permits are cut (2 tier A, 18 tier B). Because the Frederick layer carries neither valuation nor a use field, a Weld-only config flag (`LOCAL_DESC_USE_HOLD`) also holds tier A permits whose description says retail, mercantile, restaurant, well pad and the like at B3a (4 rows: two retail core-and-shell permits, a mercantile tenant finish, an oil and gas well pad); the flag is off for Adams and Denver and both rebuild byte for byte.
4. **County incentive agreements are scored; the assessor sales and personal property accounts are side lists.** On the first pass the agreements were a side list (Matthew's first decision); after the report he chose to add the signal. All 18 agreements now carry 20 points, a `planned` stage when the hearing is within 24 months, and the facility address from the agreement text (shown, since a county record naming the facility is a site, not a home address). The assessor sales (188 flagged) stay beside the list; the 34 ranked rows that match one by exact street address carry the sale in `next_step` as possible.
5. **Web checks.** 60 top-ranked rows from the second build, then the 15 incentive-agreement companies not already checked, were checked on the web for what the company makes at the site (one or two lookups each; `web_checks_weld.md` lists the source for every one). 55 were confirmed and applied through `WEB_CHECKS` in the config (25 strong, 12 moderate, 18 weak); 19 could not be confirmed in two lookups and keep the pipeline's own fit (`WEB_NOT_CONFIRMED_V3`); one (an unattached address) had nothing to check. The incentive checks matter because the name-word fit rule read BAE Systems, Microvast and Aqua-Hot Heating Systems as office or energy names. Fit points: strong +15, moderate +6, unknown 0, weak -15. The downgrades are the Weld noise profile the air-permit rule cannot see: gravel pits and dirt mines (J-2 Contracting's Sunset pit, Asphalt Specialties' Marcovich pit, SVW Farms, Colorado Sand), rail transload and fuel terminals (USRL Hudson, Cando Rail, Western Proppants), a dairy digester (CEA Greeley), a landfill (Waste Management), medical waste (Stericycle), a bulk fuel plant (Agfinity), a dairy, a battery storage site, a water utility, a plumbing contractor and a fire district.
6. **Web drops.** 5507 Distilling: the UCC collateral text itself places the distillation equipment on Fifth Street, Loveland (Larimer County), and the Weld zip is a mailing address. On the first pass the `WEB_DROPS` hook reached UCC tier A and Mile High CRE rows only, so the row stayed at rank 8; the hook now reaches tier B liens where the county config sets `WEB_DROPS_TIER_B = True` (Weld only), and the row is dropped (1 drop, counted in the report).
7. **Mailing-address trap.** 23 rows sit in the "county unconfirmed" section (`county_basis` = Weld mailing address, county unconfirmed). Eclipse Engineering (rank 21, 2540 S Main St, Erie) is in the main section on its zip share but Erie straddles Boulder and Weld and the parcel's county was not confirmed; Legacy Pie (rank 22) has Denver shops and no Windsor production site on the web.
8. **Person-named entities.** None of the top 60 is a private individual's name; `PERSON_NAMED` is empty for Weld. Registered entities that carry a founder's name (Jim's Automotive Machine Shop, C. Simpson Farms) are businesses and stay.
9. **Local noise cuts.** The water-permit airport cut (`WAT_AIRPORT`) removed 3 rows (airport construction and airline facilities). No other Weld-specific cut was added; the Denver cuts removed, among others, 87 water permits flagged on acreage alone (schools, parks, housing, roads, pipelines), 45 transit and public-works sites, 63 rows of the May to July 2024 industrial stormwater renewal wave (kept as identity evidence), 15 fuel stations and 8 emergency-generator-only air permits. 11. **No Atlas Copco data; no contact with anyone; no records requests; no browser-only portal opened; no personal names.** OEDIT code names are never decoded; the 8 OEDIT rows are "possible" at most. The step 2 ground-truth corrections are not applied.

## Method

1. Scope records to Weld County (assumption 1). 2. Cut known noise, counted per cut (`v3_report_run3.md`). 3. Resolve records to companies by normalised name and address, keeping every name variant (239 company accounts; 4 co-debtor merges, 3 same-address leading-word merges; one documented tie, Hirsh Precision, in `KNOWN_TIES`). 4. Attach address-only records (permits, site plans) to companies by address: 38 addresses named from county permit rows (the Frederick layer's parcel business name, carried as the tenant role), 3 from a state permit operator, 32 attached to a company as possible, 3 by the documented tie, 15 with one unflagged name as a possible occupier, 219 left as their own candidates. A contractor is not the account. 5. Pull every row for each candidate from every source, flagged or not (312 support rows not listed on their own). 6. Score tier A and tier B signals together under the fixed table; rank. 7. Name unattached addresses from the EPA registry (none matched). 8. `verify_v3.py`: 0 problems.

## Review

An independent review agent audited the third build against the skill and the raw data (`review_findings.md`). Verdict: fail, one blocker, five should-fix, five notes; every score, county basis, web check and ground-truth mark it recomputed passed. What changed in the fourth build:

1. **Blocker, fixed.** Hirsh Precision (C35) sat at rank 326 in the unconfirmed section while rank 159 was its exact address from the Frederick layer, not joined. Fixed with a documented tie in the config (`KNOWN_TIES`: exact name plus address on the Frederick layer and the county agreement) and a resolver rule that a company tied to the county's own permit layer is county confirmed. Adams and Denver rebuild byte for byte.
2. **Loader tiers, fixed.** Four Frederick permits (two retail core-and-shell, a mercantile tenant finish, a well pad) scored as tier A construction because the Frederick layer carries the use only in its description. Held at B3a by a Weld-only config flag (assumption 3).
3. **"Occupant" role, fixed.** The Frederick scrape wrote `occupant` for the parcel business name, a role the resolver does not read; 129 rows now carry `tenant` (the brief's role for a named occupier), and 38 addresses carry a possible occupier instead of 15.
4. **Side list labels, fixed.** A match on street address alone is now "possible", not "confirmed".
5. **5507 Distilling, fixed in build 5.** The web check places its equipment in Loveland; the drop hook now reaches tier B liens where the county config turns it on, and the row is dropped.
6. **Unconfirmed-section labels, not fixed.** Three Mile High CRE press addresses the geocoder could not match (Loveland, Boulder, Johnstown mailing cities) and 13 split-city rows carry the scripts' standard "Weld mailing address, county unconfirmed" wording; the wording is Denver's and the rows are in the right section.
