## Rules carried from Denver and Adams

1. **Match classes.** `confirmed` = the entity or the site is tied by something exact: company name plus site address, exact address plus suite, or a document naming both. `possible` = right company but another address, a generic record at the address with no name, a differing legal name with no document tying it, or a near-miss address. `none` = searched, nothing. A wrong match is worse than a miss: when unsure, use `possible`.
2. **Record date** = the record's own date (filed, applied, opened, received, recorded, meeting), not when it became visible. Say which date field you used. A source with no record date at all (an active-only layer with status only) cannot score lead time; say so and treat it as undated.
3. **Lead time** = (record date minus earliest press date) in days / 30.44, in months. Negative = source fired first. Lead time means months before or after the first press story. It does NOT mean months before a compressor purchase, and no write-up may say that.
4. **Window.** A record counts if dated from 36 months before press to 12 months after the project's latest milestone. Older or later records go in notes, not in the count. For an expansion at a plant that already existed (Agilent), an in-window record is `possible` at most unless it names the project.
5. **Noise sample.** For each build-candidate source, count records opened in one or two sample months (use September 2025 and March 2026; for an undated snapshot layer, sample 50 to 200 current records instead), and estimate the share that are plainly not industrial (housing, roofing, signs, retail, schools, roads, oil and gas well pads). Query the real layer; do not estimate from a description.
6. **Join quality.** Say which field did the matching (address, applicant name, description text, parcel, OCR text) and whether the layer carries any company name at all.
7. **Verdicts:** `build`, `park` or `drop`, one line of reason each, and a `basis` label: `back-tested`, `inherited + spot-checked` (a source of the same type as a Denver or Adams build source, such as an ArcGIS permit mirror or planning-case mirror, that you spot-checked but which holds no ground-truth project), or `spot-checked only`. Never present an inherited verdict as a tested one. Paid sources, press feeds, undated reference layers (zoning, Tier II, nonconforming use), agenda feeds you did not parse, and browser-only portals are NOT tested: list them as "not tested" with the reason.
8. **Browser-only portals (Accela Citizen Access, eTRAKiT, CommunityCore, Citizenserve, the new Accela Public Portal).** No automated collection and no agent browsing of them. Do the terms check only (robots.txt, any terms, disclaimer or privacy page) and report what the terms say in a few lines. Treat them as manual lookup sources. The Weld County legacy ACA answers a plain GET on CapDetail.aspx; that is still a portal: do the terms check and at most fetch ONE record page as a sample to describe what a detail fetch would return. Do not crawl it.
9. **Terms on the Weld County AGOL items.** The discovery notes say the Weld AGOL items carry a line saying the product was developed solely for internal use by Weld County, while being shared publicly as open_data. Quote the exact license text from the item JSON (`https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/<id>?f=json`, fields `licenseInfo` and `accessInformation`) in your notes for every Weld County AGOL source you test. Do not decide the question; report it.

## How to query

ArcGIS REST layers: use the documented `/query` endpoint with `where`, `outFields=*`, `f=json`, `resultRecordCount` and `resultOffset` paging, at most 2,000 rows a request, one request a second, user agent `step3-research-validation/1.0`. Prefer `returnCountOnly=true` for counts. Frederick's server needs `DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD'` literals in `where`. Laserfiche WebLink: the JSON services the discovery notes describe (FolderListingService, SearchService field search on the Commissioner Records template, DocumentService OCR text), with a session cookie from one GET of the WebLink home page; read-only, one request a second. CivicClerk: build the OData URL by hand (urlencode mangles `$filter` and the parentheses). PDFs: download to `/home/claude/atlasscraper/step3-validation/weld/raw/` (create it; it will be git-ignored) and parse with pdftotext or Python. Use python3 `urllib` or `curl` with a 20 second timeout from the shell; the hosts were reachable at discovery. If a host refuses (403, Cloudflare, Akamai), record that and stop; never work around it. Search by street number plus street name (addresses are inconsistently formatted: try `LIKE '%7051%EAGLE%'` style patterns on every address field and on description or applicant fields; county roads appear as CR 8, WCR 8, County Road 8, Weld County Road 8), and by company name words on any name, applicant, owner, grantee or description field. Download nothing in bulk beyond what the test needs.

## Boundaries

No form submissions, no accounts, no records requests, no contact with anyone. No personal names in any output (applicant, grantor, grantee and planner fields can hold individuals: write "individual" instead; the Weld Planning Cases view embeds the planner's name and email in Description and two fields, never copy them). No Atlas Copco data. No em dashes anywhere in the output. Query the real data; never fill a gap with a guess. Write files with the Write tool, not shell heredocs.
