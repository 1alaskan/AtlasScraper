# Adams County scrape, group `thornton` (Thornton building permits and development projects)

Pulled 2026-10-31 (UTC) per `scrape_brief_shared.md`. Scripts in `scripts/` (`thornton_common.py` is shared by both pulls); raw JSON pages under `raw/thornton_building_permits/` and `raw/thornton_development_projects/` (git-ignored).

## Assumptions (read first)

- Both layers sit on `maps.thorntonco.gov` (ArcGIS Enterprise mirror of Harris CityView). The host has no robots.txt (404) and no published terms or copyright text on either service; the CityView portal itself is robots-disallowed and was not touched. Treated as public record data; requests paced at one a second with user agent `step4-research-pull/1.0`. Total requests this run: 13 (permits: 4 counts, 4 data pages, 4 left-out counts, 1 address lookup; projects: 1 count, 1 page; plus 3 metadata reads).
- Dates are epoch milliseconds converted in UTC (same convention as the Denver pulls). DateEntered carries a time of day, so a permit entered after about 5 pm Mountain can land on the next calendar day.
- Residential means BuildingUse = RES on the permits layer. Right of Way permits (BuildingUse null) are also left out: they carry no description, valuation or work type, half are at homes with the homeowner as the only text, and the only field that could carry signal is the address point's BUS_NAME. Both counts are reported below.
- "Still active" for the 2023 carry-in means Status in Open, Under Review, In Plan Check, Pending, Permit(s) Issued, Reactivated, Returned for Correction, Submittals Incomplete, Waiting for Payment, Waiting for Submittals or Final Processing. Closed, Finalled, File Closed, Warranty, Expired (all forms) and Denied are not active.
- One permit can be mirrored to several address points (one per unit of a multi-tenant building). One row per permit number is kept, preferring the point with a street address and no unit; `address_points` says how many points the permit had.
- The permits layer has no zoning or land-use field, so the brief's "industrial" test is a word in the description, work type, business name, legal owner, subdivision or notes (searched in that order; `industrial_word` and `industrial_word_field` say what fired). Generic words were narrowed after a sample review: `process` and `equipment` only count in industrial phrasings (process equipment, process piping, owner-furnished equipment, manufacturing equipment and so on), and `manufactured` or `pre-fabricated` on their own do not count. TypeOfWork values High Piled Storage, Tank (including generator tanks), Hazardous Material, Generator Commercial, Boiler, Refrigeration Unit and Storage/Warehouse count on their own.
- Tier A requires valuation 250,000 dollars or more plus an industrial word. When the permit is a trade or accessory permit rather than Commercial New, Addition or Alteration, the rule string says so (`A: ... (trade or accessory permit)`, 29 rows) so step 5 can weight them separately.
- company_name on permits is the address point's BUS_NAME (role `occupant`) when present, else LEGALOWNER (role `owner`). LEGALOWNER is the assessor's current owner joined live, not the owner at permit time. BUS_NAME is the business recorded on the address point and can be a previous tenant.
- company_name on development projects is the cleaned Applicant (role `applicant`) when a firm survives the person filter, else the project Name (role `project`). Name is a staff-written project label and can itself contain a company (Izzio Bakery, Kiosk TI, Santa Monica Seafood).
- Personal names. Owner names that read as a person (no business word, up to five plain words, or a personal trust) are blanked (36 rows). BUS_NAME values with a professional title or a first name and surname are blanked (14 rows). Applicant strings on the projects layer are split on separators and only the firm parts kept (83 of 303 strings had a person part dropped). Free text (Description, Comments, NOTES) is scrubbed of CityView staff stamps (`[user @ date time]` and `user date time`), emails, phone numbers, `Contact <Name> ...` phrases, titles plus surname, `<Name>/DE` style references, and first-name based person names using a built-in list of about 400 common first names (street names such as Grant, Holly, Madison and Logan were removed from the list so addresses survive). 77 permit descriptions and 30 project descriptions carry `[name removed]`. A surname used alone, a first name not on the list, or a company named after its owner can still appear; a spot read of the remaining "per X" and "with X Y" phrases found only companies, documents and streets.
- No em dashes: the cleaner turns em and en dashes from the source into hyphens.

## Thornton building permits

**Source:** City of Thornton, Building Permits (2017 to present), `https://maps.thorntonco.gov/citydevweb/rest/services/Permits/MapServer/1` (CityView mirrored to ArcGIS Enterprise). Route: documented ArcGIS REST `/query`, paged by `resultOffset` ordered by OBJECTID, 2,000 rows a page. No bulk download exists (the layer is not on the Thornton open data hub with a download, and the CityView portal is browser-only and robots-disallowed); the REST query is the layer's own interface. Cheaper routes did not apply.
**Verdict:** pass (back-tested in step 2: Izzio, Kiosk, Santa Monica Seafood and Solid Power all appear with permits entered 2 to 10 months before press, and the description names the tenant on the capex permits).
**Terms and robots:** `maps.thorntonco.gov/robots.txt` is 404; the service and layer carry no copyright text, description or licence. The CityView portal (`cityviewportal.thorntonco.gov`) disallows everything except Home and Logon and was not opened. No limit on automated access or reuse is stated anywhere for the GIS mirror.
**Query shape:** this server answers any ORed clause with an HTML 500 page (`BuildingUse <> 'RES' OR BuildingUse IS NULL` failed, as ORed address LIKEs did in step 2). Every request is therefore a DateEntered range ANDed with a single `BuildingUse = 'COM'` or `BuildingUse = 'SCH'` clause, and the status filter for the 2023 carry-in is applied client-side. `http_json` treats an HTML body as a failure and stops after three.
**Rows:** 1,996 written: 1,962 permits entered 2024-01-01 to 2026-10-28 plus 34 still-active permits entered in 2023. Source's own counts for the same filters: DateEntered >= 2024-01-01 and BuildingUse COM 1,920 features, SCH 67 features (1,987 features = 1,962 distinct permit numbers after collapsing 25 extra unit address points); 2023 COM 650 features (SCH 0) = 627 distinct permits, of which 34 are active and 593 were left out (491 Closed, 45 Expired, 57 Expired Closed). Left out as residential: 20,870 (2024 onward) plus 4,164 (2023) = 25,034. Left out as Right of Way: 1,037 (2024 onward) plus 343 (2023) = 1,380. Type mix of the written rows: Fire Protection 700, Commercial Alteration 352, Sign 288, Electrical 204, Plumbing 141, Mechanical 124, Commercial New 82, Commercial Miscellaneous 51, Temporary Use 29, Commercial Accessory 16, Commercial Addition 9.
**Date range:** 2023-01-06 to 2026-10-28 (date entered). Every month from January 2024 is present, 31 to 96 rows a month (about 58 a month); July 2026 is the low month at 31. The 2023 rows are sparse by design (active only). No future dates.
**Flagged:** 414 Y of 1,996. `A: valuation 250k+ + industrial word` 86 (57 on Commercial New, Addition or Alteration; 29 on trade or accessory permits such as fire sprinklers, tanks, generators and boilers). `B3a: valuation 50k to 250k + industrial word` 54. `B3b: valuation under 50k or none + industrial word` 274 (mostly fire protection tanks, high-piled storage, alarms and sprinklers in warehouses, generators at cell sites). Evidence field for the flagged rows: description 219, type_of_work 123, bus_name 28, legal_owner 26, subdivision 16, notes 2. Everything else N with a blank rule. The ground-truth sites all flag: Izzio (15107 Grant St, three tier A rows), Kiosk/Posiflex (251 E 126th Ave), Solid Power (14902 Grant St Unit 120, SP2.5 12.0M TI), Corden Pharma, Target distribution center (15450 Washington St), Pulse 25, Crossroads 25, Creekside and the LPC Mile High shells.
**Null rates:** company_name 5.9% (117 rows: no BUS_NAME and no entity owner, including 36 owner names blanked as individuals), site_address 2.9% (58 rows, early applications with no address point yet), record_date 0%, zip 2.9%, valuation 0% blank but 0 on many trade permits, latitude/longitude 2.8%, description 0%.
**Duplicates:** 0 on record_id after collapsing unit address points (48 extra points in the fetched features; 10 written permits had more than one point, for example one permit on 11 unit points at 12295 Pennsylvania St).
**Fields:** permit_type, type_of_work, work_class (001 new, 002 addition, 003 alteration), building_use (COM or SCH), status, date_issued, date_expiration, co_date, valuation, square_feet (empty on nearly every row), bus_name, legal_owner, comm_type, notes, unit, parcel_pin, adco_pin (Adams assessor PIN), subdivision, ura, address_key, zip_source, industrial_word, industrial_word_field, address_points, latitude, longitude, parser_version. Not requested: EnteredBy (staff), Contact* (applicant mailing address), LEGALDESC, LOT, BLOCK, Name and Comments (empty on every commercial row).
**Join key:** site_address (Thornton format `15107 GRANT ST`, unit as `UNIT 120` or `STE 110`, never a comma) plus adco_pin (12-digit Adams PIN, present on 97% of rows) and address_key (CityView address id, joins to the Addresses layer and to ADDRKEY on the development projects layer where that is populated). Latitude/longitude on 97%. Company names are free text; match on address first and search `description` second, as step 2 found.
**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_thornton_building_permits.py` (about 20 seconds; `--since YYYY-MM-DD` pulls only that window and skips the 2023 carry-in, `--fresh` ignores the same-day cache, `--no-zip-fill` skips the Addresses layer call). Re-pull the last 90 days each time: DateIssued lags DateEntered by months, valuation and status change after entry, and address points are assigned late.
**Supporting Addresses layer:** one POST query (`ADDRKEY_1 IN (...)`) to `Permits/MapServer/0` for the permits without a zip. Only 3 of the 58 zip-less permits carry an address key and none of them had a zip on the address point either, so the query filled 0; 2 zips were filled from another permit at the same street address instead. The remaining 56 have no address at all in the source. The layer is not worth more than this one call.
**Gotchas:** (1) ORed clauses return HTTP 500: one clause per request, date-range plus one equality, filter client-side. (2) The description is a running staff log as well as the scope of work; names and timestamps are scrubbed but the text can still read oddly (`[name removed]` markers, voided and reactivated notes). (3) BUS_NAME is the address point's business, not the permit applicant; it is populated on 53% of commercial rows (not "usually empty" as the brief expected) and can be stale. (4) Valuation is 0 or blank on most trade permits, so tanks, racking and generators sit in B3b regardless of size. (5) DateEntered after about 5 pm Mountain shows as the next UTC day. (6) The layer id (1) and field names could change when the City republishes; the script checks field names and stops on drift.
**Not pulled:** residential permits (25,034), right-of-way permits (1,380), 2023 permits that are no longer active (593), and permits before 2023. Applicant and contractor names are not in this layer (the Contact* fields are a mailing address and were not requested).

## Thornton development projects

**Source:** City of Thornton, Development Projects, `https://maps.thorntonco.gov/citydevweb/rest/services/DevelopmentProjects/MapServer/0` (the feed behind the public Development Projects map app). Route: ArcGIS REST `/query`, the whole layer in one request (303 rows). No bulk download exists; the CityView planning portal is browser-only.
**Verdict:** pass as the earliest city-level filing (step 2: named all four Thornton ground-truth projects, fired 3 to 11 months before press on the two that filed before announcement). Weak on addresses: the DPA subtype (206 of 303 rows) has no PermitAddress or parcel, so geometry is the site join.
**Terms and robots:** same host as above, no robots.txt, no terms or copyright on the service. Staff fields (Planner, Engineer, created_user, last_edited_user) and the internal V_Drive_Link were not requested.
**Rows:** 302 written; the source holds 303 and 1 row (OnMapDate null, created_date 2023-03-27, a Presubmittal development permit row from before the bulk load, last edited 2026-08-19) is before the window. Every row with a 2024-01-01 or later date is included, OnMap Y 185 and N 117 (rows persist after OffMapDate; the app hides them, the layer does not delete them). record_date is OnMapDate (260 rows, date_type `on map`) or created_date when OnMapDate is null (42 rows, `created`, the Presubmittal subtype). 95 rows carry exactly 2024-01-01, the bulk load; their real filing dates are earlier and unknown.
**Date range:** 2024-01-01 to 2026-10-23. After the bulk-load month, 1 to 18 rows a month with no empty month from May 2024 on (February to April 2024 are empty: nothing was added between the bulk load and May 2024). No future dates.
**Flagged:** 302 Y, all `all planning and development cases`. `industrial_use` = Y on 31 (28 Non-Residential, 2 Mixed Use, 1 Institutional): 126th and Washington phases 1 and 2, Pulse 25, LPC Mile High One, Altus 86th and Grant, Washington Square warehouse/showroom, Amazon canopy at 900 E 128th, Sack Creek, Synthetic Grass Warehouse, Ferguson TI, City Creek Logistics, Terra Partners annexation at 15000 Washington, Ace Thermal Systems TI, Crossroads 25, Washington Datacenter, BSM Business Park, Bull Crossing (OCC Industrial, two cases plus the 2026 warehouses), ICM, Pinnacle flex condos, Sturgeon Electric, Kiosk TI, 9150 Pecos, plus three weak hits (Fire Station 8 "processing", Thorncreek Church "warehouse", North End Station "flex"). Words searched: industrial, warehouse, manufacturing, distribution, logistics, flex, data center, cold storage, fabrication, processing, production, across Name, Comments, Description, PAName, DevelopmentType and PermitType.
**Null rates:** company_name 0% (applicant 195 rows, project name 107), site_address 64.2% (108 rows have a street: PermitAddress on the permit subtype, ProjectLocation on some DPA rows), record_date 0%, zip 71.9% (only PermitAddress carries one), description 0%, latitude/longitude 0% (every row has a point), parcel_number 71.5% blank.
**Duplicates:** 0 on record_id and 0 on global_id. record_id is the planning PermitNumber when present (96 rows) else `GID-<GlobalID>` (206 rows); `global_id` is the stable append key.
**Fields:** industrial_use, industrial_word, global_id, object_id, project_name, applicant, permit_type, permit_status, development_type, phase, status, status_alt, approval_track, on_map, on_map_date, off_map_date, created_date, last_edited, case_numbers (the related CityView case numbers, useful for a manual portal lookup), planning_area, parcel_number, acres, project_location, housing_type, sf_units, mf_units, website, address_key, latitude, longitude, first_seen_at, last_seen_at, in_layer_last_run, parser_version.
**Join key:** latitude/longitude (every row) to the permits layer points or Adams parcels; parcel_number (13-digit, 86 rows) and address_key (88 rows) where the permit subtype carries them; case_numbers link related CSP, DP, plat and infrastructure cases. Company via applicant or project_name free text.
**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_thornton_development_projects.py` (one request, a few seconds). Append-only: every run snapshots the whole layer, appends rows whose GlobalID is new, refreshes on_map, off_map_date, phase, status, status_alt, permit_status, last_edited, last_seen_at and in_layer_last_run on rows already held, and marks rows that have left the layer `in_layer_last_run = N` without deleting them. Weekly is right (about 20 new rows a month, and rows may roll off). To rebuild from scratch delete the CSV first. `--since` only limits which new rows are added; `--fresh` refetches today's snapshot.
**Gotchas:** (1) 95 rows share the 2024-01-01 bulk-load date, so lead time cannot be measured on them. (2) The Applicant field is a person on about a quarter of rows; the cleaner keeps only firm parts and the applicant column is blank where only a person was given (company_name then falls back to the project name). (3) Comments repeat the Description on the permit subtype and carry "contact the planner" sentences on the DPA subtype; those are scrubbed. (4) Website is the old cityofthornton.net project page on 2024 rows and a CityView StatusReference link on newer ones; neither was fetched. (5) The subtype split (PermitNumber present or not) changes what fields are filled; do not treat blank PermitAddress as missing data.
**Not pulled:** nothing in scope is missing. Planner and engineer names, created and edited user names and the V drive link were deliberately not retrieved. Attachments (the layer supports them) were not queried.

## QA

Both CSVs start with the exact 17 columns in order; flag is only Y or N; no description over 500 characters; no row dated before 2023-01-01 (permits) or 2024-01-01 (projects); no em dashes; 0 duplicate record_ids; row counts match the source's own counts for the same filters after the documented client-side steps (unit address points collapsed, 2023 inactive dropped, one pre-2024 project row excluded). Spot checks of 50 company_name values on each file found firms, public bodies, schools and churches only.
