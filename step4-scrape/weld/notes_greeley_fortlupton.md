# Step 4 (scrape), Weld County, group `greeley_fortlupton`

Run 2026-11-06 (UTC), following `datasource-validator-scraper`. Two sources, both planning-case snapshot layers that keep no history: Greeley TRAKiT projects (discovery rank 5) and Fort Lupton planning projects (rank 8). Scripts in `scripts/` (`gf_common.py`, `pull_greeley_trakit_projects.py`, `pull_fort_lupton_planning_projects.py`), raw JSON and HTML under `raw/<slug>/snapshot_<day>/` (git-ignored).

## Assumptions (read first)

1. **Snapshot semantics.** Both sources are live views of open cases. Every run reads the whole layer (and, for Fort Lupton, the whole page), appends rows never seen before and never deletes. Rows already held keep their record_date, company_name, description, retrieved_at and first_seen_at; only the status columns and last_seen_at are refreshed. A case that drops out keeps its row with `in_layer_last_run` (Greeley) or `in_source_last_run` (Fort Lupton) = N. History starts today.
2. **Date floor.** The brief's scope is 2024-01-01 onward, but the task was to pull every current case, so the scripts default `--since 2023-01-01` (the QA floor) and keep the three 2023-dated cases the sources still list: Greeley SPR2023-0009 (applied 2023-03-01, approved, Boomerang Self Storage) and Fort Lupton PPL2023-004 and FPL2023-004 (referral 2023-10-04, withdrawn). Nothing is dated before 2023.
3. **Flag rule.** Every row is flagged Y, rule `all planning and development cases`, per the brief. `industrial_use` follows the brief's word list exactly (industrial, warehouse, manufacturing, distribution, flex, data center, processing, plant, compressor station, oil and gas facility; the misspelling INDISTRIAL is caught). A second column `industrial_adjacent` (with `industrial_adjacent_word`) marks the wider net the list does not name: business park, outside or outdoor storage, storage yard, equipment, gravel, mining, aggregate, pipeline, midstream, oil and gas, truck, fleet, renewables, solar, battery, substation, pump station, self storage, lot consolidation. The analysis can decide whether to score it; by the brief's rule alone Fort Lupton has zero industrial_use rows even though EquipmentShare, Northland Business Park and the Everist mining cases are there.
4. **Private individuals.** APPLICANT is a person on most Greeley rows (132 of 157 filled) and on part of the Fort Lupton rows (9 strings of the form "Person, Firm, LLC" reduced to the firm; 2 blanked). Project names that read as a person's name or carry an individual applicant's surname were blanked (Greeley 4: two owner-named plats, one lot line adjustment, one lot consolidation). A staff first name in one Greeley note was replaced with "staff". PLANNER and EMAIL were not requested from the Greeley layer; the Fort Lupton page's "Planner:" column was not carried; Doc_Link is reduced to its last path component (`doc_folder`) so no drive or user folder names are kept. Residual risk: a surname inside a subdivision name (platted subdivision names are place names and were kept).
5. **company_name.** The applicant when it is a company (role `applicant`), else a business named in the project name (role `project`: Walmart, Culver's, 7 Brew, Loveland Industries, Sphere Renewables and the like, found by a brand list plus a capitalised-phrase-with-corporate-suffix rule), else blank (role `unknown`). Residential subdivision names (Vista Meadows, Sweetgrass Meadows, Lancaster Crossing) are not treated as companies. Greeley applicant companies are mostly consultants (engineering, landscape architecture, sign contractors), not end users; the end user, where known, is in `project_name`.
6. **No zip, no zoning.** Neither source carries a zip or a zoning field; both columns are blank or omitted. Greeley zoning cases carry the zoning in the description only.
7. **Nothing behind a login was opened.** Greeley eTRAKiT (`case_link`) redirects every case search to its login page (302 observed); the link is kept for a rep with the free public account, not fetched. Fort Lupton DocumentCenter folders were not opened (see the Fort Lupton card).
8. No Atlas Copco data, no contact, no records requests, no form submissions. Not committed to git.

## Source card: Greeley TRAKiT projects

**Source:** City of Greeley "ProjectMapData" layer, `https://gis.greeleygov.com/arcgis_svr/rest/services/Data_Services/TrakitProjects/MapServer/0` (CentralSquare TRAKiT planning cases mirrored to ArcGIS Enterprise). Slug `greeley_trakit_projects`.

**Route:** ArcGIS REST `/query` (route 2, documented API). No bulk download exists; the eTRAKiT portal (route 5) is login-walled; the CivicClerk packets (rank 9) carry only some cases. One request of 172 rows (maxRecordCount 1000), ordered by ESRI_OID, `returnGeometry=false` because LON and LAT are attribute fields. User agent `step4-research-pull/1.0`, one request a second.

**Terms and robots:** `gis.greeleygov.com/robots.txt` returns 404 (no rule). The service carries no copyrightText, serviceDescription or license. Not a Weld County AGOL item, so the Weld `licenseInfo` clause does not apply. The eTRAKiT disclaimer (step 2) is liability only. Treated as public data with no stated restriction.

**Verdict:** pass (built). The layer carries a real APPLIED date on every row, a parcel on every row and an address on 85 percent; the kill criteria do not fire. It cannot be back-tested because it holds no history (step 2).

**Row counts:** 172 written, source count 172 (`returnCountOnly`), 0 left out. Nothing residential was dropped: the brief allows leaving residential rows out, but the layer is kept whole as a snapshot (the residential and non-development cases are visible by `record_type`: 41 PUBLIC RECORDS REQUEST zoning letters, 13 ADDRESS, 8 HISTORIC PRESERVATION DESIGN REVIEW, and so on).

**Flagged:** 172 by `all planning and development cases`. industrial_use Y on 6: SPR2025-0027 (industrial flex building, 2334 117th Ave), SPR2026-0018 (outside storage yard added to an existing industrial use, 1299 H St), SPR2026-0019 (Loveland Industries site improvements, 14520 CR 64), SUB2026-0026 (merging lots into a single industrial lot, 2301 1st Ave), SUB2026-0003 (Osborn Industrial Park lot consolidation, 1859 2nd Ave), ZON2026-0001 (Great Western Industrial Park establishment of zoning). industrial_adjacent Y on 10 (gravel storage yard SPR2026-0003, outside storage PUD2026-0005, self storage, solar, two telecom equipment upgrades, three lot consolidations, a pump station). Two rows worth a manual look that no rule catches: SPR2024-0023 "SPR Amendment - Building Expansion" at 330 E 8th St (industrial corridor, applicant an individual) and SPR2026-0010 "Storage building addition" at 2340 117th Ave (next door to the SPR2025-0027 flex building).

**Null rates:** company_name 115 of 172 blank (67 percent; 26 applicant companies, 31 businesses read from the project name); site_address 26 blank (15 percent, mostly annexations, rezones and city projects); record_date 0; parcel_number 0; latitude and longitude 0.

**Dates:** APPLIED 2023-03-01 to 2026-11-03. Monthly counts: 1 in 2023, 5 in 2024, 33 in 2025 (30 of them November and December), 133 in 2026. The gaps (2023-04 to 2024-04, mid 2024, 2025-03 to 2025-08) are roll-off of closed cases, not missing data: this is an active-case view.

**Duplicates:** none; PROJECT_NO and UID are both unique across the 172 rows. The script stops on a duplicate PROJECT_NO.

**Fields carried:** the 17 shared columns (record_id = PROJECT_NO, record_date = APPLIED, date_type `applied`, record_type = PROJECTTYPE, city Greeley, zip blank), then industrial_use, industrial_word, industrial_adjacent, industrial_adjacent_word, record_subtype (PROJECTSUBTYPE), status, parcel_number, applicant (company only), project_name (scrubbed), applied_date, submitted_date, approved_date, latitude, longitude, case_link (eTRAKiT, login required), object_id, uid, first_seen_at, last_seen_at, in_layer_last_run, parser_version. Not carried: PLANNER, EMAIL, COORD_X, COORD_Y, COORDINATES (state plane duplicates of LAT and LON).

**Join key:** site_address (TRAKiT style: no W or E prefix on numbered streets, so 2400 29TH ST is W 29th St; blank on most annexations and rezones), parcel_number (Weld parcel id, filled on every row), latitude and longitude. company_name on 33 percent.

**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_greeley_trakit_projects.py` (reads today's cache if present), `--fresh` to refetch, `--since YYYY-MM-DD` to add only newer cases. Weekly: 156 of 172 cases were applied since 2025-11, so cases roll off within months.

**Gotchas:** active-only, so a case disappears when closed and the snapshot is the only history. Applicant is an individual on 84 percent of filled rows and is blanked; the consultant firms that remain are not end users. The one record before 2024 (SPR2023-0009) is kept as the snapshot rule says. Status values are TRAKiT workflow states (WITH APPLICANT, SUBMITTED, ACCEPTED, UNDER REVIEW, APPROVED, COMPLETE, CONSTRUCTION, PUBLIC HEARING, INCOMPLETE SUBMITTAL). The sibling TRAKiT2 USR polygon layer holds only 2010 to 2016 oil and gas cases and was not pulled.

**Not pulled:** eTRAKiT case pages (login), CivicClerk packets (rank 9, parked in step 2), the Public Space permits layer (rank 21, parked).

## Source card: Fort Lupton planning projects

**Source:** City of Fort Lupton `Planning_AGOL` feature service on ArcGIS Online, `https://services2.arcgis.com/7fqvVMRB6DcB5wXC/arcgis/rest/services/Planning_AGOL/FeatureServer` (layer 1 polygons, layer 0 points, 37 features each, item `77fbc0728e40475088e10eb3ca212fc1`, owner `ftluptongis`), merged with the Current Development Projects page `https://www.fortluptonco.gov/463/Current-Development-Projects` (CivicPlus, four tabs: Under Review 13 rows, Approved 14, Denied 0, Withdrawn 2; 29 rows carrying 33 case numbers). Slug `fort_lupton_planning_projects`.

**Route:** ArcGIS REST `/query` for the layers (route 2; one request each, `returnCentroid=true` on the polygons, geometry on the points) and a single HTML GET for the page (route 5, used because the page is the only place the newest cases appear: the layer was last edited 2026-07-28 and the page already lists SPR26-002 Sphere Renewables Lot 3, SUP26-006 and ADM26-001, which the layer lacks). The page is one request at the robots Crawl-delay of 10 seconds. No bulk download, no JSON behind the page.

**Terms and robots:** `services2.arcgis.com/robots.txt` returns 403 (read as no stated rule for the REST endpoint). The AGOL item has `licenseInfo` null, `accessInformation` "City of Fort Lupton GIS", access public, no copyrightText on the service; not a Weld County item, so the Weld `licenseInfo` clause does not apply. `fortluptonco.gov/robots.txt` (200): for every user agent it disallows `/admin`, `/Search`, calendar, RSS and similar paths, allows `/463/` and `/DocumentCenter/`, and sets `Crawl-delay: 10`; a second block names AI crawlers (ClaudeBot among them) with the same disallow list. Our user agent is not on that list, and the only path fetched is allowed. The DocumentCenter folder index pages (`/DocumentCenter/Index/NNNN`) are React shells whose file list loads from `/Admin/DocumentCenter/Folder/GetDocumentsForAFolder` (found in the page's script bundle), a path robots disallows, so no folder listing or submittal PDF was fetched. The page answered our user agent directly (200, 134 KB); the plain-curl resets step 1 saw did not recur.

**Verdict:** pass (built). Dates on 32 of 40 rows, company applicants on 31, a case-number key that joins the layer to the page and to the DocumentCenter folder. No history, so not back-testable (step 2). The site_address null rate (88 percent) is the weak field.

**Row counts:** 40 written: 37 layer features (36 also on the page, 1 layer-only: CPL25-001 High Pointe Hills concept plan, approved 2025) plus 3 page-only cases (ADM26-001 South Platte Valley Historical Society site plan, SPR26-002 Sphere Renewables Lot 3 site plan, SUP26-006 reservoir special use permit). Source counts: 37 polygons, 37 points, 29 page rows. Page case numbers not written as their own row: LUP2022-0022, the layer's Project_Num alias of FPL2022-0003 (Vista Meadows filings 3 and 4), merged by alias. 0 residential rows left out (kept whole as a snapshot; the residential plats are visible by name and `record_type`).

**Flagged:** 40 by `all planning and development cases`. industrial_use Y: 0 (no row's text uses a word from the brief's list). industrial_adjacent Y: 9: ADM26-004 EquipmentShare administrative site plan at 145 14th St (under review, referral 2026-08-12), COZ25-003, LLA26-001 and PPL26-002 Northland Business Park (KCP Northland, change of zone approved 2025-12-02, lot line adjustment and fourth filing plat under review since 2026-07-16), SUP25-002 and ALC25-001 LG Everist mining (approved), SUP26-002 Western Midstream Parsnip pipeline (under review), ADM26-003 AT&T equipment at 1325 Factory Circle (approved), SPR26-002 Sphere Renewables Lot 3 (page only, under review, snapshot date). Also of interest with no word hit: COZ25-002 and ADM25-002 at 2183 S Denver Ave (Vecchiarelli Holdings, approved 2025), SUP26-003 Xcel Energy, ANX26-001 and SUP26-001 County Road 8.5 (withdrawn).

**Null rates:** company_name 7 of 40 blank (18 percent); site_address 35 blank (88 percent; the 5 filled come from the project name or the folder name: 2183 S Denver Avenue twice, 145 14th St, 1325 Factory Circle, 100 McKinley Ave); record_date 0, but 8 rows carry the retrieval date with date_type `snapshot` (5 layer features with no date and the 3 page-only cases); latitude and longitude blank on the 3 page-only rows.

**Dates:** record_date is the earliest of Ref_Response_Date (31 rows), PC_Date (1 row) and CC_Date on the layer, else a hearing date printed on the page, else the retrieval date. Range 2023-10-04 to 2026-11-06. By year: 2023 2, 2025 14, 2026 24 (8 of them snapshot-dated). Gaps are roll-off, not missing data.

**Duplicates:** two case numbers appear twice by design (FPL25-002 Lancaster Crossing filings 1 and 2, FPL2022-0003 Vista Meadows filings 3 and 4: one feature per filing). De-duplicate on `record_id` if one row per case is wanted. Layer rows key on record_id plus project_name; page-only rows key on record_id alone and are upgraded in place when the layer later carries the case.

**Fields carried:** the 17 shared columns (record_id = Plan_Num, or the case number read from Doc_Link when Plan_Num is blank, as on ADM26-003; record_type = Plan_Code; city Fort Lupton; zip blank), then industrial_use, industrial_word, industrial_adjacent, industrial_adjacent_word, plan_type_name, project_name, applicant (company only), status (layer Status, else the page tab), approved, ref_response_date, pc_date, cc_date, year, comments, doc_folder, web_link (DocumentCenter folder), page_section, page_hearing_text, latitude, longitude (point layer, else polygon centroid), source_detail (layer, page or both), object_id, first_seen_at, last_seen_at, in_source_last_run, parser_version. Not carried: On_Website, the *_Text date strings, Shape fields, the full Doc_Link path, the page's planner column.

**Join key:** record_id (case number, shared by the layer, the page and the DocumentCenter folder name); company_name on 82 percent; latitude and longitude on 37 of 40; site_address only where the name carries it. No parcel field.

**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_fort_lupton_planning_projects.py` (`--fresh`, `--since` as above). Weekly: the page is edited in place by staff (two fetches 15 minutes apart on 2026-11-06 differed: SUP26-006 was renamed from "Brighton Water Augmentation Reservoir" to "Lupton Meadows Reservoir Special Use Permit" and SPR26-002 gained the words "Site Plan"), and the layer lags the page by months.

**Gotchas:** the layer has no address field and no submittal date (Ref_Response_Date sits a few weeks after submittal). Plan_Num is blank on one feature and duplicated on two pairs. Applicant mixes "Person, Firm" strings; the firm is kept. The page's hearing column holds TBD on most under-review rows and one typo (05/14/2025 for a 2026 hearing, taken from the layer instead). "Xcel Enerygy (C/O PSCo)" is the source's spelling, kept as given. Page-only rows have no coordinates and a snapshot date until the layer catches up.

**Not pulled:** DocumentCenter folder listings and submittal PDFs (robots disallows the `/Admin/` path the listing comes from; the folder links are in `web_link` for a rep), the Agenda Center (rank 25), the point layer's attributes beyond geometry (identical to the polygon layer's).
