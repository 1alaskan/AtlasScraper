# Weld County scrape notes, group `county_agol`

Pulled 2026-11-06 from the cloud shell, user agent `step4-research-pull/1.0`, one request a second, 2,000 rows a page, raw pages cached under `step4-scrape/weld/raw/<slug>/` (git-ignored). Four Weld County ArcGIS Online hosted layers on `https://services.arcgis.com/ewjSqmSyHJnkfBLL/ArcGIS/rest/services/`. Shared helpers are in `scripts/weld_agol_common.py` (paging with a per-page raw cache, key-batched lookups, the contact scrub, the individual-name screens, Ownership-table joins, and the first_seen / last_seen snapshot files). Parser version `county_agol-1.0`.

## Assumptions

- Route: documented ArcGIS REST `/query` on every layer (route 2 in the skill). No bulk download was needed: the Sales and SPR layers advertise `Extract`, but a paged query is the same data and resumable; the Planning Cases view and the Ownership table are query only.
- Two of the four layers carry no date (the active Planning Cases view and the Ownership table), so they are snapshot sources: `record_date` = the first day this pipeline saw the row, `date_type` = `snapshot`. Each has a `snapshot_<slug>.csv` next to the output with `first_seen` and `last_seen`; a row that leaves the layer is kept in the output with `active` = N. Nothing is deleted. The first run (2026-11-06) dates everything to today, so first_seen only becomes a signal from the second run on.
- The SPR layer's `DATE_` is the plat recording date (`date_type` = `recorded`); the Sales layer's `SALEDATE` is the deed date (`date_type` = `sale`), not the recording date, and the layer lags recording by a few weeks (latest sale seen 2026-10-21 on 2026-11-06).
- Weld's assessor codes unincorporated situs as `PROPERTYCITY` / `LOCCITY` = `WELD`; written as `Unincorporated Weld County`. Other city values are title-cased as the layer gives them (so `Longmont`, `Brighton` and `Erie` rows are the Weld County parts of those towns).
- Parcel numbers are 12 digits. The SPR layer stores `PARCEL__` as a double and loses the leading zero; it is zero-padded before the Ownership join. Parcel 0 means no parcel on the polygon (6 of 49 SPR rows).
- Names: the assessor separates parties with `;`. A multi-party name keeps only the parts that read as companies (`NINE TEN HOLDINGS LLC; DEKOWZAN THEODORE` becomes `NINE TEN HOLDINGS LLC`); a name that is one person, or a personal trust, is blanked. `&` is not a separator (it sits inside company names such as `BAE SYSTEMS SPACE & MISSION SYSTEMS INC`). The screen is conservative: a two to five word name with no business term is treated as a person. Planning case titles are kept as `company_name` only when a word is left after the generic use vocabulary is removed (`Gilbert Contracting, Inc.` yes, `Contractor Shop` no).
- The Planning Cases view embeds the county planner's name and a mailto link in its `Document` field and carries `Planner` and `PlannerEmail` fields. None of the three is requested. The `Description` field was clean in all 96 rows but is scrubbed anyway (HTML, `Planner: ...` tails, e-mails, phones). The Ownership table's mailing street fields (`ADDRESS1`, `ADDRESS2`, `CAREOF`) are never requested; only the mailing city and state are kept.
- No zip anywhere: none of the four layers carries a situs zip (the Ownership `ZIPCODE` is the mailing zip and is not used). `zip` is blank on every row.

## Source card: Weld County planning cases (rank 2)

**Source:** Planning Cases for Development Activity Map view, `.../Planning_Cases_for_Development_Activity_Map_view/FeatureServer/0`, ArcGIS REST query (`1=1`, ordered by FID, 96 rows in one page). Active cases only.
**Verdict:** pass (snapshot). Undated and address-less by design; the dated signal is the first_seen diff from now on.
**Files:** `scrape_weld_county_planning_cases.csv`, `snapshot_weld_county_planning_cases.csv`, `scripts/pull_weld_county_planning_cases.py`.
**Rows:** 96 written, source count 96, 0 left out. Types USR 48, ZPAG 24, SPR 18, COZ 4, SIF 2 (one `USR ` with a trailing space normalised). Status Under Review 57, Conditionally Approved 25, Under Construction 14.
**Flags:** all 96 Y, rule `all planning and development cases`. `industrial_use` Y on 44 (I-1, I-2 or I-3 zoning in the description, or an industrial, warehouse, distribution, data center, compressor station, processing or plant word), N on 52 (solar farms, BESS, kennels, event centers, mining, contractor shops, C-3 storage yards).
**Null rates:** company_name 81 percent blank (18 of 96 titles name a company or project; 78 titles are use descriptions such as `Contractor Shop` and are kept in the `title` column instead), site_address 100 percent blank (the layer has none; `latitude` and `longitude` are filled on every row), record_date 0 (all 2026-11-06, snapshot).
**Date range:** 2026-11-06 only. No gaps to report; the snapshot file will carry the history.
**Duplicates:** none (CaseID is unique).
**Fields carried:** 17 shared columns, then `industrial_use`, `parcel_number` (filled for 7 of the 18 SPR cases by joining the recorded SPR layer on case number; the view itself has no parcel), `zoning` (parsed from the description on 95 of 96; both districts on a change of zone, for example `A, I-2`), `status`, `case_link` (the Accela CapDetail URL from `Epermit`), `latitude`, `longitude`, `title`, `case_type_code`, `first_seen`, `last_seen`, `active`, `spr_recorded_date`, `parser_version`.
**Join key:** CaseID to the SPR layer's `B1_ALT_ID`; the Accela capID inside `case_link`; lat and long. No parcel for USR, ZPAG, COZ and SIF cases.
**Refresh:** `python3 step4-scrape/weld/scripts/pull_weld_county_planning_cases.py` daily or weekly; rows never seen before are appended with today's first_seen, rows gone from the view flip to `active` = N. `--since YYYY-MM-DD` also writes the rows first seen on or after that day to `scrape_weld_county_planning_cases_since_<date>.csv`. `--fresh` refetches today's page.
**Gotchas:** descriptions run to 947 characters (trimmed to 500 after zoning is parsed from the full text). The `Category` field is the status. `Show` and `Type` have trailing-space variants. Titles with a typo (`Car Dealersehip`, `Commercia Junkyard`) are still use descriptions, not names. The planner contact lives in `Document`, not `Description`; keep both `Document` and the two planner fields out of `outFields` if the script is ever rewritten.
**Not pulled:** closed cases (the view holds active ones only), the Accela record pages behind the `Epermit` links.

**Source:** Site_Plan_Review_open_data, `.../Site_Plan_Review_open_data/FeatureServer/0`, ArcGIS REST query `DATE_ >= DATE '2023-01-01'` with `returnCentroid=true` and no polygon geometry (49 rows, one page), plus two key-batched joins: Ownership table on `PARCELNB` (43 parcels, 40 matched) and the Planning Cases view on `CaseID` (8 still active).
**Verdict:** pass. Dated and parcel keyed; the Ownership join supplies the owner, business name and situs that the layer lacks.
**Files:** `scrape_weld_county_site_plan_reviews.csv`, `scripts/pull_weld_county_site_plan_reviews.py`.
**Rows:** 49 written, source count 49, 0 left out. 2023: 15, 2024: 8, 2025: 10, 2026 (to September): 16. 20 are amendments (`1AMSPR...`, `2AMSPR...`, `3AMSPR...`), 29 new SPRs.
**Flags:** all 49 Y, rule `all planning and development cases`. `industrial_use` Y on 28 (zoning I-1, I-2 or I-3, or an industrial word in the joined description, business name or tenant accounts), N on 21 (C-3, PUD, A, R-5 and one blank zoning).
**Null rates:** company_name 20 percent blank (39 of 49 filled from the Ownership owner `NAME`, falling back to `BUSINESSNAME`; 6 rows have parcel 0 so nothing can join, 3 parcels have no Ownership row, 6 owner or tenant name fields were blanked as individuals), site_address 27 percent blank (36 situs addresses from the Ownership row), record_date 0.
**Date range:** 2023-02-01 to 2026-09-16. Roughly one recording a month; 14 months in the window have none (2023-06, 2023-09, 2023-12, 2024-01, 2024-04, 2024-06, 2024-08, 2024-12, 2025-03, 2025-05, 2025-06, 2025-08, 2025-09, 2025-12), which is normal for a layer this small.
**Duplicates:** none (`B1_ALT_ID` is unique; `CASE_NAME` sometimes carries a tail such as `1AMSPR25-92-183, 93` and is not used as the key).
**Fields carried:** 17 shared columns, then `industrial_use`, `parcel_number`, `zoning`, `acreage`, `case_number`, `reception_number`, `amendment`, `business_name` (assessor BUSINESSNAME on the real account, companies only), `tenant_accounts` (personal property accounts on the parcel, companies only; 16 rows), `owner_account`, `owner_account_type`, `subdivision`, `lots`, `section_township_range`, `permit_status`, `planning_status` and `title` (from the active cases view, 8 rows), `latitude`, `longitude` (polygon centroid), `parser_version`.
**Join key:** 12-digit parcel to the Ownership and Sales layers; case number to the Planning Cases view and to Accela.
**Refresh:** `python3 step4-scrape/weld/scripts/pull_weld_county_site_plan_reviews.py [--since YYYY-MM-DD] [--fresh]`; a `--since` other than the default writes `scrape_weld_county_site_plan_reviews_since_<date>.csv`. Weekly is plenty.
**Gotchas:** `PARCEL__` is a double with the leading zero lost (padded here) and is 0 on 6 rows. One 1899 placeholder date exists in the layer (outside the filter). `ZONING` can hold two districts (`C-3, I-1`) and has leading-space variants. The supplementary projects from step 2 are both present: EcoMod SPR25-0010 (EM PROPERTY LLC, 22303 Interstate 76 Frontage Rd, I-3, recorded 2026-02-04) and TruLog 3AMSPR25-18-0004 (TRU LOG, 20684 Niobrara Blvd, I-3, recorded 2026-01-21).
**Not pulled:** the 386 recordings before 2023, polygon geometry (centroid only).

## Source card: Weld County assessor property sales (rank 4)

**Source:** Sales_Point_open_data, `.../Sales_Point_open_data/FeatureServer/1`, ArcGIS REST query `SALEDATE >= DATE '2024-01-01'` with point geometry (36,482 rows, 19 pages) plus a 2023 carry-in query (`SALEDATE` in 2023 AND land type Industrial or an industrial occupancy or building description, 242 rows, one page). No joins: the layer carries parties, price, deed, land type, property type, occupancy, building description, size, acreage and situs in one row.
**Verdict:** pass (back-tested in step 2: 5 of 6 ground-truth projects had an in-window sale, all before press).
**Files:** `scrape_weld_county_property_sales.csv`, `scripts/pull_weld_county_property_sales.py`.
**Rows:** 2,250 written from 36,482 in the 2024+ filter plus 242 carry-in candidates (234 kept, 8 dropped because the server `LIKE` matched a non-industrial string). Left out and counted: 26,140 residential sales (SaleType Residential 16,033 houses, 4,896 vacant residential lots, 1,729 manufactured homes, 930 townhouses, 745 condos, 677 duplexes, 574 vacant ag lots with a planned single-family occupancy, 389 vacant lots with a residential occupancy code, 61 apartment buildings, 39 triplexes, the rest small). Bulk subdivision lot transfers (vacant, no occupancy, more than 10 accounts on one reception, almost all developer-to-developer deeds such as 4,305 Peakview FG 1 lots in Evans) were collapsed from 8,369 rows to 43 reception rows marked `bulk_lot_transfer` = Y with the lot count, land types and total acreage in the description.
**Flags:** 188 rows Y, rule `sale 2M+ on industrial or commercial-industrial parcel` (140 distinct receptions; 182 of the 188 carry a company grantee); 2,062 N. `industrial_use` Y on 784 rows (land type Industrial, or a Warehouse, Equipment Building, Outdoor Storage, Hangar, Quonset, Industrial or Manufacturing occupancy or building description; `Mini Warehouse`, `Warehouse Discount Store`, manufactured and mobile homes excluded; a farmhouse whose secondary occupancy is an equipment building does not count).
**Null rates:** company_name 34 percent blank (grantee is a private individual on 785 rows, whole or part; 1,485 of 2,250 rows name a company grantee), site_address 42 percent blank (vacant land has no situs; 1,305 filled), record_date 0. Grantor filled on 1,382 rows (companies only).
**Date range:** 2023-01-04 (carry-ins) to 2026-10-21. 2024: 699, 2025: 702, 2026: 615, 2023 carry-ins: 234. Every month from 2024-01 to 2026-10 has rows (27 to 96 a month); October 2026 is partial because the layer lags recording.
**Duplicates:** none on `record_id` = RECEPTIONNO/ACCOUNTNO. A multi-account sale is one row per account with the same price and reception (`accounts_in_sale` says how many), so sum prices by reception, not by row.
**Fields carried:** 17 shared columns, then `sale_price`, `grantor`, `grantee`, `occupancy_code`, `occupancy_description` (all three occupancies), `building_sf`, `acreage`, `parcel_number`, `reception_number`, `deed_code`, `latitude`, `longitude`, `account_number`, `accounts_in_sale`, `sale_type`, `land_type`, `property_type`, `building_description`, `construction_class`, `commercial_sf`, `year_built`, `subdivision`, `neighborhood`, `valuation_sale`, `industrial_use`, `carry_in`, `bulk_lot_transfer`, `parser_version`.
**Join key:** parcel and account number to the Ownership table, reception number to the recorder, situs address plus city (upper case, county roads as `COUNTY ROAD 8`), grantee company name to state registrations.
**Refresh:** `python3 step4-scrape/weld/scripts/pull_weld_county_property_sales.py --since <last record_date>` writes `scrape_weld_county_property_sales_since_<date>.csv` for the new rows (de-duplicate on record_id downstream; the default run refetches the whole window into a new day-stamped cache). Weekly.
**Gotchas:** there is no `Manufacturing` occupancy code; manufacturing plants show as occupancy `Warehouse` with building `Indust Lght Manufacturing` or `Indust Heavy Manufacturing`. `OCCCODEDESCRIPTION1` on a vacant lot is the planned use (`Single Family Residential`), which is how vacant residential lots were recognised. `PROPERTYCITY` `WELD` is unincorporated. `GRANTOR` and `GRANTEE` hold private individuals often (blanked). Spot checks: BAE Systems Space & Mission Systems 2026-10-05 $30.0M at 4120 Specialty Pl (flag Y); Eastman Park Industrial LLC 2025-06-17 $23.0M, 297,753 sf, 9952 Eastman Park Dr; FNLR Concrete Jungle LLC 2026-04-08 $42.6M, 127,085 sf heavy manufacturing, 2145 E Crown Prince Blvd Brighton; M4 Elevation 25 I LLC 2025-06-17 $43.5M for two light-manufacturing buildings on Elevation Dr, Mead.
**Not pulled:** residential sales (counted above), sales before 2023, 2023 sales that are not industrial.

## Source card: Weld County assessor personal property accounts (rank 12)

**Source:** Ownership table, `.../Ownership2/FeatureServer/17`, ArcGIS REST query `ACCTTYPE = 'Personal'` (2,452 rows, two pages). Resolver snapshot.
**Verdict:** pass as a resolver (not a signal). Undated; from the second run on, a new `P` account number is a dated "equipment declared at this site" event.
**Files:** `scrape_weld_county_personal_property_accounts.csv`, `snapshot_weld_county_personal_property_accounts.csv`, `scripts/pull_weld_county_personal_property_accounts.py`.
**Rows:** 2,202 written of 2,452 in the source filter; 250 left out because neither the business name nor the owner name reads as a company (sole proprietors declared under a personal name). 779 name fields were blanked as individuals across the kept and dropped rows.
**Flags:** all 2,202 N, rule blank, as the brief says.
**Null rates:** company_name 0 (1,981 from `BUSINESSNAME`, the rest from the owner name), site_address 3.7 percent blank (81 rows with no situs street, mostly telecom and leasing accounts keyed to a city only), record_date 0 (all 2026-11-06, snapshot). `owner_name` filled on 2,143 rows, parcel on 2,183.
**Date range:** 2026-11-06 only. Account numbers run P0000019 to P9111042; the sequence is the only ordering of age.
**Duplicates:** none (ACCOUNTNO is unique).
**Fields carried:** 17 shared columns, then `account_number`, `parcel_number`, `situs_city`, `mailing_city`, `mailing_state`, `owner_name` (the taxpayer, companies only), `business_name`, `subdivision`, `first_seen`, `last_seen`, `active`, `parser_version`. Situs cities: Greeley 707, Windsor 181, Frederick 118, Fort Lupton 113, unincorporated 108, Erie 106, Longmont 99, Firestone 94.
**Join key:** situs address plus city (the street name holds the direction on some rows: `W 29TH` against `29TH`), parcel number, business and owner name.
**Refresh:** `python3 step4-scrape/weld/scripts/pull_weld_county_personal_property_accounts.py` monthly; new accounts are appended with that day's first_seen, accounts that leave the table flip to `active` = N. `--since YYYY-MM-DD` also writes the rows first seen on or after that day to `..._since_<date>.csv`.
**Gotchas:** `BUSINESSNAME` is a DBA or site label and can be generic (`SALES OFFICE`, `HIGHLAND OFFICE`, `ASPHALT PLANT`); the owner name is the legal taxpayer. Equipment lessors (Varilease, Marlin, GreatAmerica, Holam) file P accounts at their customers' sites, which is itself an equipment-in-place tell but names the lessor, not the plant. `BUSINESSNAME` `0` is treated as blank. Only `ACCTTYPE = Personal` was pulled; the `Leasing` (1,479) and `Industrial` (806) account types noted in step 2 were not, per the brief.
**Not pulled:** mailing street addresses, the other account types, the Account Inventory table.

## QA done

Every CSV starts with the 17 shared columns in order; `flag` is Y or N only; no description over 500 characters; no record dated before 2023-01-01; no duplicate record_id; no e-mail, mailto or em dash in any field; 60 random company names from the sales file, 60 from the personal property file, and every name in the planning and SPR files were read and none is a person. Row counts match the source's own count for every filter (96, 49, 36,482 plus 242, 2,452) before the documented local exclusions. Reruns read the cache and do not refetch.
