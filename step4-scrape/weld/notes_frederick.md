# Weld County scrape, group `frederick` (Frederick building permits and development applications)

Pulled 2026-11-06 (UTC) per `scrape_brief_shared.md`. Scripts in `scripts/` (`frederick_common.py` is shared by both pulls; the applications script also imports the permit script for its company-name screens); raw JSON pages and the HTML snapshot under `raw/frederick_building_permits/` and `raw/frederick_development_applications/` (git-ignored). About 50 requests in all, including the exploratory ones, at one a second with user agent `step4-research-pull/1.0`.

## Assumptions (read first)

- **The permit layer step 2 tested is incomplete; a sibling layer is the full mirror.** `Accela_Building_Permits/FeatureServer/3` (the table layer in the discovery row and the step 2 notes, 19,578 rows) drops Accela-era permits once their status is Closed (Complete, Withdrawn or Denied): it holds 1,019 permits opened since 2024-01-01, all with open statuses. `Building_Permits/MapServer/0` (a point layer, 24,538 rows; `Accela_Building_Permits_for_Notebook/FeatureServer/0` is the identical layer under another name) holds 2,387 permits opened since 2024-01-01 and is a strict superset (every PermitId in the table is in it; the 1,368 extras are 1,205 Closed - Complete, 69 Closed - Withdrawn, 6 Closed - Denied and 88 null-status drafts). The pull uses the point layer. Same 13 fields on both, so the step 2 back-test transfers.
- **Fields the layer carries:** FullAddress, PermitId (Accela internal id, `FREDERICK-REC24-00000-0004D`), CustomId (the permit number, `BLDG24-0086`, `CONDUSE-25-000003`, `25TMP-001001`), PermitType, Status, Description (4,000 chars), UpdateDate, StatusDate, OpenedDate, ClosedDate, ParcelID, Name (the site address with zip, sometimes followed by a project title), and point geometry. **No valuation, no square footage, no applicant, contractor or owner, no issued date, no zoning.** So `valuation` and `issued_date` are blank on every row, `square_feet` is parsed from the description where it states one (30 rows), `finaled_date` is ClosedDate, and zip comes from the Name field.
- **Supplements joined on the server:** the sibling parcel layer `Accela_Building_Permits/FeatureServer/0` (assessor attributes per parcel: ACCTTYPE as `land_use`, NAME as `parcel_owner`, BUSINESSNA as `parcel_business`, SUBNAME, GIS_Acres, situs) by ParcelID, and the town's `Zoning_Designation/MapServer/0` polygons (9,008 with a ZONE_CODE) by point-in-polygon on the permit point. Industrial zoning = I, LI-FLEX (`LI-FL`), HI-FLEX (`HI-FL`) and BLI (Business Light Industrial).
- **Dates** are epoch milliseconds in UTC. OpenedDate carries no time of day. `record_date` = OpenedDate, `date_type` = `opened`.
- **Residential** means PermitType starting with `Residential`; counted and left out. The `Plans Review Application` type (14 rows) is the builders' single-family master plan submittals and is left out too. Six of the town's own Accela test records at town hall (401 Locust St: `testest`, `yrdyryd`, `Test Commercial New Construction Permit`, `Test Sign Permit Application`, `TEst`, `COMMERCIAL FEE ESTIMATOR`) are left out and listed by the script.
- **Still active** for the 2023 carry-in means Status not matching Closed, Finaled, Cancelled, Expired, Void, Denied or Withdrawn (null status counts as active).
- **Temporary application ids.** 32 rows carry a `25TMP-`/`26TMP-` CustomId (Accela `EST` records: an application saved before submittal, status null). They are kept with `application_stage` = `temporary application id (unsubmitted draft)`; nine of them have a later permit with the same address and opening text and carry that permit number in `superseded_by`. Count a scope once.
- **company_name** is a tenant or project name read from the text (role `tenant`, or `project` for a code name such as Project Endeavor): a known company name anywhere in the title or description; the Accela convention of the business name leading the description before two or more hyphens (`CHASE BANK -- Construction of ...`); the Name field's project title; or a `for <Company>` capture. When the text gives nothing, the parcel's assessor business name is used with role `occupant` (129 rows; it is the business recorded on the parcel, which can be a landlord's tenant list such as `GREAT CLIPS/DOMINOS/COLD STONE/WING STOP` or a previous occupant). Every candidate must carry a business word or be a known chain; one- to four-word plain names with no business word are treated as possible persons and dropped (`Lyons`, `Puckies`, `ClawCade` were lost this way, which is the safe side of the rule). `parcel_owner` is the assessor owner and is blanked when it reads as a person (shares and `(JT 45%)` fragments stripped first); a `Firm, Person` business field keeps only the firm.
- **No em dashes:** the cleaner turns em and en dashes from the source into hyphens; e-mail addresses and phone numbers are removed from free text (none were found).
- Both scripts stop on repeated errors, check field names before pulling, and raise on a row-count mismatch against the layer's own count.

## Frederick building permits

**Source:** Town of Frederick, Building Permits (Accela mirrored to the town's ArcGIS Enterprise 12.1 server), `https://gis.frederickco.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Building_Permits/MapServer/0`. Route: documented ArcGIS REST `/query`, paged by `resultOffset` ordered by OBJECTID, 2,000 rows a page, `DATE 'YYYY-MM-DD'` literals. No bulk download exists (the town's open data hub lists the GIS catalog, not this Accela mirror); the Accela Citizen Access portal is browser-only and was not opened. Cheaper routes did not apply.
**Verdict:** pass (back-tested in step 2 on the sibling table layer: X26 Agilent confirmed on four permits naming Project Endeavor or the new manufacturing building, first at +1.9 months after press). This pull adds a tie for C35 Hirsh Precision: the parcel business field puts `HIRSH PRECISION PRODUCTS INC` at 4300 Godding Hollow Pkwy, where the layer holds a 2023-03-09 sign permit "on renovated portion of existing building" and two 2026-07 roofing permits (all B3b on HI-FLEX zoning). The 65,000 sf building itself still has no permit in the mirror in 2021 to 2023, as step 2 found.
**Terms and robots:** `gis.frederickco.gov/robots.txt` is 404; the services carry no copyright or licence text. The town's GIS Terms of Use (`https://www.frederickco.gov/769/Terms-of-Use`, read 2026-11-06) say: "You can copy, distribute, transmit and adapt the data in this GIS catalog freely", ask for a hyperlink to the open data site where possible, and disclaim accuracy ("as is", no warranties, user assumes all risk). `www.frederickco.gov/robots.txt` allows everything but admin and search paths to `User-agent: *` (Baidu and Yandex are disallowed). No Weld County AGOL item is involved, so the county `licenseInfo` line does not apply.
**Query shape:** three count queries and two data queries did the pull (`OpenedDate >= DATE '2024-01-01' AND (PermitType NOT LIKE 'Residential%' OR PermitType IS NULL)`, and the same for calendar 2023), plus one POST `PARCEL IN (...)` lookup of 174 parcels and ten pages of zoning polygons. The server accepted ORed and NOT LIKE clauses without trouble.
**Rows:** 432 written: 395 permits opened 2024-01-01 to 2026-11-05 plus 37 still-active permits opened in 2023. Source's own counts for the same filters: 413 non-residential since 2024-01-01 (less 14 Plans Review Application and 4 test records = 395) and 108 non-residential in 2023, of which 39 are still active (less 2 test records = 37) and 69 were left out as finished (60 Closed - Complete, 5 Permit Expired, 4 Closed - Withdrawn). Left out as residential: 1,974 (2024 onward) plus 2,912 (2023) = 4,886; plus 14 Plans Review Application rows (residential master plans) and 6 test records. Type mix of the written rows: Commercial Alteration 118, Sign 84, Commercial Electrical 57, Commercial New Construction 48, Commercial Mechanical 25, Plug and Abandon 18 (oil well plugging, conditional-use records), Commercial Roofing 16, Temporary Structure 14, Commercial Accessory Structure 13, Commercial Addition 10, Commercial Plumbing 8, Commercial Foundation 6, Commercial Solar/PV 6, Commercial Demolition 3, Water Tap 3, Communication Facility 2, Grading 1. Sub-permits keep their own `record_type`; `permit_class` groups them (new, addition, alteration, foundation, accessory, demolition, grading, trade or accessory).
**Date range:** 2023-01-30 to 2026-11-05 (opened). Every month from January 2024 is present, 3 to 26 rows a month (about 11.5 a month; July 2024 is the low month at 3, July 2026 the high at 26 with the Agilent Area J and Lab rows). The 2023 rows are sparse by design. No future dates.
**Flagged:** 233 Y of 432. `A: construction permit + industrial word (no valuation in layer)` 45 (alteration 24, new 17, addition 3, foundation 1; evidence words: core and shell 8, warehouse 8, lab 5, manufacturing 3, mezzanine 3, piping 2, then plant, tank, tanks, pallet racking, racking, cold storage, industrial, oil and gas, distribution, flex, canning, substation, BESS, fabrication once each). `B3b: no valuation, non-residential on industrial property` 175 (zoning 124, land use 29, an industrial word in the text on a permit that is also on industrial property 20, subdivision named Industrial 2). `B3b: no valuation, industrial word on trade or accessory permit` 13 (fabrication, BESS, asphalt, walk-in cooler, generator, refrigeration, tank, silo, battery storage). Everything else N with a blank rule (199: downtown and Highway 52 retail, restaurants, signs, dealerships, the Silverstone Marketplace shells, well plugging, town facilities). B3a cannot fire without valuation. The ground truth flags: all four 2023 Agilent Endeavor permits (three tier A, the trailer N), the 2026 Agilent Area B lab renovation phases 1 and 2-4 (A), the Area J equipment, piping and electrical work (A), Area B and D room renovations (B3b); Hirsh (B3b); BLDG26-0585 and its draft 26TMP-000877 22,476 sf office and warehouse building at 5520 Iris Pkwy (A); the Spindle BESS control building, Tesla and Clearway maintenance buildings at 6300 Wheatland Blvd (A); Quantum Canning at 3540 Hwy 52 (A on the construction documents, B3b on the canning-machine electrical work).
**Null rates:** company_name 50.0% (216 rows: no name in the text and no business on the parcel; 24 of the 45 tier A rows are among them), site_address 2.5% (11 rows: drafts and a few trade permits with no address point), record_date 0%, zip 15.3% (the Name field is blank on 66 rows), parcel 2.1%, latitude/longitude 2.3%, zoning 3.0%, land_use 4.6%, status 9.7% (42 rows, the drafts and newly opened records), description 1.9%, square_feet 93.1% blank, project_title 88.9% blank.
**Duplicates:** 0 on record_id (CustomId, else PermitId). The 32 temporary application ids are distinct records in Accela; nine duplicate a later permit's scope (`superseded_by`).
**Fields:** permit_type, permit_class, application_stage, superseded_by, status, status_date, issued_date (always blank), finaled_date (ClosedDate), updated_date, parcel (12-digit Weld parcel), valuation (always blank), square_feet (from the description), zoning (town ZONE_CODE at the point), land_use (assessor ACCTTYPE: Commercial 265, Exempt 62, Industrial 47, Agricultural 18, Real State Assd 10, Vacant Land 9, Residential 7, blank 20), parcel_owner, parcel_business, subdivision, parcel_acres, industrial_word, industrial_word_field (description, project_title, land_use, zoning, parcel_business or subdivision, searched in that order), project_title (the Name field's project label, 48 rows), accela_record_id (PermitId), latitude, longitude, parser_version. Not requested: nothing else exists on the layer.
**Join key:** site_address (Accela format `7051 EAGLE BLVD`, unit as `UNIT B`; a handful of rows use mixed case or append the town name, which the cleaner strips) plus parcel (97.9% of rows; the stable key for history, since step 2 found applicant mailing addresses in FullAddress on pre-2022 rows) and latitude/longitude (97.7%). Company names are free text; match on address and parcel first and search description second.
**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_frederick_building_permits.py` (about 45 seconds, 20 requests; `--since YYYY-MM-DD` pulls only that window to a separate file and skips the 2023 carry-in, `--fresh` ignores the same-day cache, `--no-zoning` skips the polygon lookup). Re-pull the last 90 days each month: Status, StatusDate and ClosedDate change after opening, and a draft `TMP` id is replaced by a `BLDG` number when the application is submitted.
**Gotchas:** (1) Use the point layer, not table layer 3 (see assumptions); anyone re-running step 2's queries against layer 3 will see a third of the volume and no closed permits. (2) No valuation anywhere in the Accela mirror, so size has to come from the description (`square_feet` when stated) or the parcel. (3) The Name field is inconsistent: usually `<address>, FREDERICK, CO <zip> : <title>` with an empty title, sometimes `<title> -- <address>`, and blank on 66 rows; zip follows it. (4) The parcel business field is the assessor's record of who is at the parcel and can be stale or a landlord's tenant list. (5) Permits opened under a temporary id have null Status and sometimes no address or description; `application_stage` separates them. (6) Plug and Abandon rows (CONDUSE ids) are oil-well plugging notices, not buildings; they are kept (not residential) and flag N. (7) The parcel layer's ZIPCODE is the owner's mailing zip, not the situs, so it was not used.
**Not pulled:** residential permits (4,886), 2023 permits no longer active (69), residential master plan submittals (14), test records (6), and permits before 2023. Applicant, contractor and owner names do not exist on the layer; the Accela Citizen Access portal (browser-only) was not opened.

## Frederick development applications

**Source:** two feeds merged. (a) Town of Frederick, Development Applications Under Review, `https://gis.frederickco.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Development_Applications/FeatureServer/0` (point layer, 22 rows, undated, with Application_Type as a review tier of Administrative, Minor or Major wrapped in an HTML link, Project_Type, Description, Project_Name, Zoning_Existing, Zoning_Proposed, Public_Hearing and a DocumentCenter folder link; Contact and Email are town planner fields and were not requested). (b) The What's Developing page, `https://frederickco.gov/855/Whats-Developing`, a CivicPlus page with three HTML tables: neighborhood meetings for projects not yet submitted (meeting date in the name), applications under review (submittal date in the name) and approved applications (approval date in the name), each with location, scope, application type, planner (not copied) and public hearing. Route: one ArcGIS REST `/query` for the layer and one GET of the page; no bulk download, API or structured export exists for the page. The page is the dated, current feed; the layer lags it (3 of its 22 rows match a page row, and the other 19 are older or stale items such as Agilent Endeavor, which the page no longer lists) and supplies zoning, a point and the document folder.
**Verdict:** pass as the earliest town-level filing (step 2: X26 Agilent appears by name on the layer, undated). First snapshot today, so no history before 2026-11-06.
**Terms and robots:** GIS host as above (no robots.txt, open data terms). `www.frederickco.gov/robots.txt` disallows only admin, search and event paths; the page is public HTML. The planner column and the layer's Contact and Email fields carry staff names and were not copied. Two project names on the page carry a landowner surname as an annexation name; those parentheticals are replaced with `(annexation)`.
**Rows:** 67 written: 46 from the page only, 18 from the layer only, 3 from both. Source's own counts: 50 page rows (24 under review, 22 approved, 4 neighborhood meetings) and 22 layer rows; one page row (Park n Ride Annexation, submitted 2023-12-26, still under review) is before the 2024-01-01 window and was skipped; two projects appear under review twice on the page (Silverstone Filing 7 as a meeting and an application) and merge into one row. record_id is a slug of the project name (`frederick-da-park-4040-replat-b-lot-2`).
**Date range:** 2024-06-10 to 2026-11-06. date_type: `submitted` 23 (page under-review rows), `approved` 22 (page approved rows carry only an approval date; it is used as record_date rather than the retrieval date, since the brief lists the date kind in date_type and an approval date beats a snapshot date), `neighborhood meeting` 4 (projects not yet submitted), `snapshot` 18 (layer-only rows, retrieval date). The page holds 1 to 6 dated rows a month from 2025-03 on, with gaps in 2024 and early 2025 because the page only shows items still under review or recently approved; it is not an archive.
**Flagged:** 67 Y, all `all planning and development cases`. `industrial_use` = Y on 23: Raspberry Hill Business Park Lot 17 (industrial warehouse and office, 8440 Raspberry Way, submitted 2025-03-24; permit 26TMP-000859 15,000 sf PEMB opened 2026-10-07), Meadowlark Business Park Filing 3 Replat C (about 20,000 sf industrial building, 5520 Iris Pkwy, 2025-09-08; permit BLDG26-0585 opened 2026-10-13), Del Camino South Lot 8 (Mile High Metal Works 20,000 sf plus industrial building, 4501 Salazar Lane, 2025-11-17), Transwest site plan amendment at 7245 Johnson Dr (approved 2025-11-19), Colorado Crane School conditional use in BLI (2026-04-06), Grantham Buildings 4 and 5 (two 4,400 sf light industrial, 3759 and 3763 Puritan Way, 2026-06-02), Forge X Workspaces (four 10,000 sf light-industrial buildings, 4700 and 4606 Falcon Pl, meeting 2026-06-22), Carbon Valley Rec maintenance building with warehouse bays (701 5th St, 2026-09-07), a preschool conditional use in BLI (zoning word only), and on the layer: Agilent Endeavor (new manufacturing and warehouse buildings north of 7051 Eagle Blvd, I district), Park 4040 Replat B Lot 2 (55,500 sf industrial flex), Bear Industrial Park Lot 4 Block 2 (Craft Solutions, 7,506 sf light industrial), Bear Industrial Park fence variance, Flight Workspaces, Full Metal Iron storage yard expansion, Golden Triangle Construction yard, Allied Auto Importers truck storage lot, Freedom Carwash (BLI), Raspberry Hill Lots 1, 4 and 5, 11 and 12 (BLI dealership and grading items). Words searched: industrial, warehouse, manufacturing, distribution, logistics, flex, data center, cold storage, fabrication, processing, production, plant, compressor, oil and gas facility, storage yard, outdoor storage, maintenance building, wash bay, truck, crane and the BLI, LI-FLEX and HI-FLEX zoning names; a well pad on its own does not count (Civitas and Oxy well pad rows are N).
**Null rates:** company_name 53.7% (36 rows; present on 31: known companies and applicants named in the project name or scope, roles `project` 29 and `applicant` 2), site_address 26.9% (18 layer-only rows have a point but no address; every page row has a location, sometimes an intersection rather than a street number), record_date 0%, zip 100% (neither feed carries one), latitude/longitude 68.7% blank (only layer rows have a point), description 0%.
**Duplicates:** 0 on record_id.
**Fields:** industrial_use, industrial_word, status (under review, approved, neighborhood meeting (not yet submitted), under review (layer only)), page_section, submittal_date, approval_date, meeting_date, application_type (page type, or the layer's Project_Type with its review tier in brackets), project_type (layer), project_name, location_text, public_hearing (page text such as `Yes. Board of Trustees 11/5/25`), zoning_existing, zoning_proposed, documents_link (the page's SharePoint folder for the submittal, or a DocumentCenter index), layer_documents_link, source_feed (page, layer, both), layer_objectid, latitude, longitude, first_seen, last_seen, in_source_last_run, parser_version. Not fetched: the document folders (SharePoint and DocumentCenter) and the hearing packets.
**Join key:** project name (business park, filing, lot and block) and the page's location text (street address on most rows); the permit layer's address and the parcel's SUBNAME tie a site plan to its later permits (Meadowlark Filing 3 Replat C to BLDG26-0585, Raspberry Hill Lot 17 to 26TMP-000859). Company via project_name or description free text. No parcel on either feed.
**Refresh:** `python3 scripts/pull_frederick_development_applications.py` (two requests, a few seconds). Append-only: every run snapshots both feeds, appends rows whose slug is new, refreshes status, dates, links and zoning on rows already held, promotes a row's record_date to the submittal date when the page later shows one, and marks rows that left both feeds `in_source_last_run = N` without deleting them. Weekly is right (the page changes with each submittal and approval, and approved rows roll off after some months). To rebuild from scratch delete the CSV first. `--since` only limits which new dated rows are added; `--fresh` refetches today's snapshot.
**Gotchas:** (1) Rows merge on project-name tokens with every number (lot, filing, block) required to agree, companies required to agree and at least one shared scope word; two applications on the same lot (Freedom Carwash on the layer, Christian Brothers Automotive on the page, both Glacier West Lot 9B; Trailer Source expansion approved and a 55,500 sf flex building under review, both Park 4040 Lot 2) are kept apart on purpose. A renamed project will appear as a new row. (2) The page's date is the only date; it is written as `(m/d/yy)` inside the project name, and a row with no date (none today) would get the retrieval date. (3) The approved table drops rows after a while and the under-review table drops them on approval, so a project can go from `submitted` to `approved` between runs; the archive keeps the submittal date. (4) The page differed from step 2's reading a day earlier (Mile High Metal Works now sits under "Del Camino South Replat Outlot A, Lot 8"), so names are not stable across edits. (5) Application_Type on the layer is a review tier (Administrative, Minor, Major) wrapped in an HTML link to the land use code, not an application type; `application_type` carries it in brackets. (6) The page parse raises if it finds fewer than 10 rows, as a layout-change guard.
**Not pulled:** the one 2023 page row (Park n Ride Annexation, 2023-12-26), the document folders, the CivicClerk hearing packets (rank 26, parked), planner names and contact fields.

## QA

Both CSVs start with the exact 17 shared columns in order; `flag` is only Y or N; no description over 500 characters; no row dated before 2023-01-01 (permits) or 2024-01-01 (applications); no em or en dashes, e-mail addresses or phone numbers in either file or in the scripts; 0 duplicate record_ids; every row's county is Weld and city Frederick; no future dates. Permit row counts match the layer's own counts after the documented client-side steps (14 master-plan rows, 6 test records and 69 finished 2023 permits removed). Every distinct company_name in both files (112 and 28 values) was read: chains, local firms, districts, churches, town facilities and one firm named after its founder (`MARK YOUNG CONSTRUCTION INCORPORATED`); no private individual. Parcel owner values were read the same way (LLCs, districts, Agilent, Kroger, Lowe's; two personal-looking owners blanked). Spot checks of ten permit rows against the live layer (Agilent 7051 Eagle Blvd series, 5520 Iris Pkwy, 6300 Wheatland Blvd, 4300 Godding Hollow Pkwy) matched on number, date, type, status and description.
