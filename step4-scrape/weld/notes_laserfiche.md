# Step 3 (scrape), Weld County, group `laserfiche`: notes

Run 2026-11-06 from the cloud shell, one source: Weld County BOCC Laserfiche WebLink, Tax Incentive Agreements (discovery rank 1). Followed `datasource-validator-scraper` (route, robots and terms, sample, build, QA). Step 2's validation (`step3-validation/weld/notes_county.md`, rank 1) already proved the signal on all six ground-truth projects, so the sample step here was a re-read of the 18 cached agreements plus a live comparison of two pages against the cache.

## Assumptions (read first)

1. **Scope is every agreement from 2022-01-01**, as the brief says for this source (the 2022 and 2023 agreements are still inside their equipment phase windows). Record date is the `Hearing Date` template field (the BOCC approval date), `date_type` = `hearing`. The packets also carry an earlier Finance pass-around date and the agreement's own execution date; neither is used because the document is not public until it is filed with the hearing.
2. **The 23 search hits are 18 agreements plus 5 Section 179D forms.** The dispatch brief described the five non-agreement hits as 30-11-123 resolutions or amendments. They are not: they are Section 179D energy-efficient commercial building deduction allocation forms for county buildings (Health Department building, Public Works Service Center, Centennial Center, North Jail Complex, Chase Building renovation at 822 7th St Greeley), with the architect or engineer that receives the federal deduction in `Names`. They match the search only because their Notes read "SECTION 179D TAX INCENTIVE". Following the wide-net rule they are written as rows with `record_type` = `179D deduction allocation`, `company_role` = `grantee`, flag N, rule blank, so the loader can tell them apart; they are not compressed-air signals. No 30-11-123 resolution or amendment exists as a separate record in this window (the resolution is page 1 of each agreement packet). The script still classifies `amendment` (Notes containing "amend") and `resolution` (DocCode RESO) for future hits.
3. **Company name is the `Names` field as the portal gives it** (clean upper case, one party per line). Where a parent and a trade name both appear they are joined with ` / ` (for example `K2D INC / COLORADO PREMIUM FOODS`). Upstate Colorado Economic Development, which is listed in `Names` on the three 2026 agreements and appears in the packet text of every agreement from mid 2023, is moved to `other_parties`. No individual appears in `Names` on any of the 23 hits; the script drops any `Names` line that carries neither a business word nor a digit.
4. **Site address comes only from the OCR** ("Taxpayer's Facility at ..." or, for BAE, "the Business Facility ... at ..."). Two certain OCR fixes are applied and documented in the script: `1-76` to `I-76` (EcoMod), and `Fredrick` to `Frederick` (Agilent, as printed in the agreement itself). One per-document override: K2D / Colorado Premium's OCR reads "2400 29`1 Street"; the street is written as `2400 W 29th Street` per step 2's reading, and `ocr_confidence_note` says so. US Engineering Metalworks' agreement names 510 Basher Drive as the Facility (the Upstate letter also mentions the existing 390 Mountain View Road plant); the agreement's address is the one written.
5. **Dollar figures and years come only from regexes anchored on the agreement's phrases, never guessed.** `phase_1_cap` is written only where the word "cap" appears (BAE Phase I, $13,765,102; BAE Phases II and III are set later by conferral, so blank). The standard Weld agreement has no cap: it rebates 50 percent of the personal property tax on equipment placed in service in a stated year or window for ten tax years, so `rebate_pct`, `term_years`, `term_tax_years` and `equipment_in_service_years` carry the real terms. The Project Information Form and Finance sheet in the 2023 to 2026 packets give estimates, written to `est_phase_1_equipment_investment`, `est_phase_2_equipment_investment`, `est_phase_1_total_investment` (the form's "Total Est. Investment (phase 1 building/land/equipment)"), `finance_sheet_new_equipment` and `est_10yr_pp_tax_due`; where the OCR is unreadable (BAE's phase 1 equipment "$ 1o1M", TFP's "$ 90 Milton") the field is blank.
6. **Small Business Incentive Program applications were not pulled** (see Not pulled). Nothing else from the Commissioner Records template was pulled.
7. No form submissions, no login, no records request, no contact with anyone, no Atlas Copco data. Nothing committed to git; the raw cache is git-ignored.

## Source card: Weld County incentive agreements

**Source:** Weld County BOCC Laserfiche WebLink, repository CTBIMAGES, Commissioner Records template. `https://minutes.weld.gov/WebLink/Welcome9.aspx?repo=CTBIMAGES`. File `scrape_weld_incentive_agreements.csv`, slug `weld_incentive_agreements`, script `scripts/pull_weld_incentive_agreements.py`.

**Route:** the WebLink JSON services, read-only. GET `Welcome9.aspx?repo=CTBIMAGES` for the `WebLinkSession` cookie; POST `SearchService.aspx/GetSearchListing` with `{[Commissioner Records]:[Notes]="*TAX INCENTIVE*"} & {[Commissioner Records]:[Hearing Date]>="1/1/2022"}` (paged 100 at a time; 23 hits in one page); POST `FolderListingService.aspx/GetDocumentInfo` (`dId` = entryId) for the true page count; POST `DocumentService.aspx/GetTextHtmlForPage` per page for the OCR text. Cheaper routes do not exist: there is no bulk export, no documented API, and no direct PDF URL on this portal (step 1 and step 2 both looked), so OCR per page is the only text route. The portal's `LF:Basic` full-text search was not used (it timed out in step 1; the Notes field search is exact). One request a second, user agent `step4-research-pull/1.0`. Requests this run: 30 on the first pass (1 cookie, 1 search, 23 document-info calls, 5 page-1 OCR calls for the 179D rows) plus 2 for the live spot check and 1 for the `--since` test; every agreement page came from step 2's cache.

**Terms and robots:** `https://minutes.weld.gov/robots.txt` is 404. `https://minutes.weld.gov/WebLink/robots.txt` answers 200 with `User-agent: * / Disallow: /MyWebLink.aspx / Disallow: /Login.aspx / Disallow: /Search.aspx` (step 2 recorded a 404 for this path on 2026-11-05; it is served now). None of the three disallowed paths is used: the script never opens `Search.aspx` (the HTML search page), `MyWebLink.aspx` or `Login.aspx`; it calls `SearchService.aspx`, `FolderListingService.aspx` and `DocumentService.aspx`, which are not listed. Terms: the portal disclaimer (recorded at discovery) is liability only, no commercial-use restriction, no login. The Weld AGOL `licenseInfo` does not apply to this source.

**Sample and spot check:** the 18 cached agreements were re-read in full; page 2 of 20220470 (Hirsh) and page 3 of 20262355 (BAE) were fetched live on 2026-11-06 and match the cached text exactly after whitespace normalisation. Hearing dates, Names and Notes come from the live listing.

**Row counts:** 23 rows written; the portal's own `hitCount` for the same search is 23. 18 `tax incentive agreement` rows, 5 `179D deduction allocation` rows (2 AGRMT, 1 CORESP, 1 FORM, 1 AGRMT dated 2025-06-09 under a 2023 document id). Left out: 0 rows. Residential rows: none exist in this source.

**Flags:** `county tax incentive agreement` 18 (every agreement names a taxpayer). N 5 (the 179D rows, rule blank). The two 2026 Upstate-sponsored agreements that no longer carry "30-11-123" in Notes (US Engineering, Motherload) are found because the search uses "TAX INCENTIVE", as step 2 advised; a second search on `*30-11-123*` returned 15, all inside the 23.

**Null rates:** agreements: `company_name` 0 of 18, `site_address` 0 of 18, `record_date` 0 of 18, `zip` blank on 14 of 18 (the agreements rarely print one), `city` 0 of 18 (Windsor 4, Frederick 3, Fort Lupton 2, Greeley 1, Hudson 2, Nunn, Platteville, LaSalle, Brighton, Johnstown, Longmont 1 each; the Brighton and Longmont sites are the Weld County parts of those cities). Extra fields on the 18: `rebate_pct` 18, `term_years` 18, `term_tax_years` 15 (Agilent and Owens-Brockway use a rolling window per in-service year; BAE's term is the 2026 to 2061 agreement term), `equipment_in_service_years` 17 (BAE: three phases, no year stated), `project_codename` 4 (Mercury, Jackalope, Krill, Hawthorn), `phase_1_cap` 1, `est_phase_1_equipment_investment` 6, `est_phase_2_equipment_investment` 3, `est_phase_1_total_investment` 7, `finance_sheet_new_equipment` 10, `est_10yr_pp_tax_due` 10, `facility_parcel` 0 (no agreement states a parcel number), `other_parties` 12 (every agreement from 2023-07 on). All 23 rows: `company_name` 0 blank, `site_address` 4 blank (179D rows with no street in Notes).

**Date range:** 2022-01-17 to 2026-09-16, hearing dates. Agreements by month: 2022-01 (2), 2022-02, 2022-12 (3), 2023-07 (4), 2024-10 (2), 2025-05, 2025-07, 2025-08, 2026-09 (3). The gaps (2022-03 to 2022-11, 2023-08 to 2024-09, 2024-11 to 2025-04, 2025-09 to 2026-08) are real: the county signs a handful of these a year, in clusters, and the unfiltered template stream (about 280 records a month, step 2) carries no other agreements of this kind. No future dates. No duplicates (23 distinct document ids and entry ids).

**Fields carried:** the 17 shared columns, then `doc_code`, `names_field` (lines joined with ` | `), `notes_field`, `page_count`, `project_codename`, `phase_1_cap`, `phase_2_cap`, `phase_3_cap`, `rebate_pct`, `term_years`, `term_tax_years`, `equipment_in_service_years`, `other_parties`, `facility_parcel`, `est_phase_1_equipment_investment`, `est_phase_2_equipment_investment`, `est_phase_1_total_investment`, `finance_sheet_new_equipment`, `est_10yr_pp_tax_due`, `laserfiche_entry_id`, `ocr_source` (raw cache, step 2 cache or live), `ocr_confidence_note`, `parser_version`. `description` is a composed one-line summary (company, codename, facility, rebate, in-service years, term, caps, estimates), cut at a separator under 500 characters. `source_url` is the record's DocView page (`DocView.aspx?id=<entryId>&dbid=0&repo=CTBIMAGES`, answers 200 after one redirect).

**Join key:** `company_name` (the Names field) against the assessor Ownership table and the other Weld files; `site_address` + `city` against the assessor situs fields and the permit layers (step 2 joined every agreement's address to Ownership this way). No parcel or account number in the template or the agreements.

**Refresh:** `python3 step4-scrape/weld/scripts/pull_weld_incentive_agreements.py` rebuilds the CSV from the cache in about two seconds; `--since 2026-09-01` runs the field search from that hearing date (one request), merges it with the cached listings and fetches only new documents' page counts and pages; `--fresh` refetches everything (about 250 requests, four to five minutes). Worth running weekly: the county files new agreements with the hearing packet within days, and the lead over press ran 0.4 to 18 months in step 2.

**Gotchas:** `thumbnailPageCount` in the search listing caps at 5, so page counts come from `GetDocumentInfo`. The agreement starts on page 2 to 8 of the 2023 to 2026 packets (Finance pass-around sheet, Upstate letter, Project Information Form, commissioner emails are filed in front of or behind it), so every page is OCR'd and parsed as one text. The OCR is image OCR of signed scans: digits inside dollar amounts are sometimes misread (left blank by the strict parser), the state is printed as `CO`, `Colorado` or both (`..., Hudson, CO 80642, Colorado`), and the 2022 scans drop or garble a character here and there. The packets also carry county and company staff names and emails in the forwarded approvals; the script writes none of them (only `Names`, `Notes` and regex-anchored fields reach the CSV); the raw cache that holds them is git-ignored. The step 2 cache at `step3-validation/weld/raw/lf_<docid>.txt` is read page by page and copied into this source's raw folder on first use, so deleting it later costs nothing.
