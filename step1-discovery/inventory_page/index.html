Level is the discovery run that found the source: national, Colorado state, or a county (Denver or Adams; pick the county with the dropdown when County is selected). A few rows carry a different tag in the source file (macro context series, two Texas state examples kept in the national list, one regional paper in the state list); that tag shows under the level. Usefulness is the 1 to 5 score assigned in discovery. "Source checked" means discovery confirmed the source exists and how it is updated, accessed and licensed; it does not mean the source was back-tested. 48 sources have been back-tested so far (5 in Denver batch 1, 23 in Denver batch 2, 20 in the Adams County run: 18 against the county's known projects and 2 sampled for one month) and show their verdict: build, park, drop, or inconclusive where the source could not be properly searched. "Use" is Y only for the sources with a build verdict from back-testing. Every other source is N, with the reason: its back-test result, or why it was not back-tested. An N for an untested source means not yet, not never.
